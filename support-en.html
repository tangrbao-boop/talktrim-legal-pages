Supported use

TalkTrim is a macOS app for preparing reviewable Final Cut Pro spoken-word rough-cut suggestions. It requires macOS 14.0 or later. TalkTrim 1.0 currently supports FCPXML 1.14 exported from Final Cut Pro, with one Project, one Primary Storyline, one main spoken-word video with its own audio, and corresponding existing captions. It does not turn arbitrary video imports into automatic completed edits and does not perform speech recognition. Caption Segmentation is a separate local feature for existing captions; it does not call AI or automatically decide what content to delete.

AI and connections

You configure third-party AI services with your own API key and pay usage charges separately from TalkTrim. Model Management lets you add service accounts and test connections or discover models where implemented; some services require manual model-ID entry. Configuration, discovery, or a successful connection does not establish production rough-cut support or editing quality.

Production rough cuts can continue to use the existing DeepSeek connection, whose default address is https://api.deepseek.com, or an explicitly selected, adapted Alibaba Model Studio account with qwen-plus. The new production integration is limited to Model Studio qwen-plus; it does not establish support for other providers, other models, or all complex projects. DeepSeek appears alongside other providers in the service list, and existing users retain its dedicated key controls.

  • Go to Settings → AI & Models → Model Management, choose a service, and add an account. For Model Studio, choose the region matching your account and key, check the HTTPS address, enter your own key, keep or add qwen-plus, and save. Check the actual operator of a dedicated or custom address.
  • Explicitly choose that account and qwen-plus in Production Rough Cut Account and Model. Adding, saving, refreshing, or testing an account does not automatically select it for production.
  • To use DeepSeek, select the built-in connection and manage its key through the dedicated area. Manage other accounts’ keys, tests, and addresses within their respective accounts. Changing an address adds no protocol support.
  • The actual address operator receives the request and authentication key and may forward them. Check its identity, fees, terms, and data rules first.

Before each generation, check the displayed account, model, and receiving address’s host information; check the full address in its settings. Approval sends caption text and IDs, selected pause/silence/boundary summaries, editing preferences, task and request parameters, and, where needed, derived intermediate editing plans and refinement context. A generation may involve parallel requests, chunks, and bounded retries. Original video, audio, and complete FCPXML files are not uploaded as AI request content. Declining prevents that generation from sending and does not delete existing local projects or results. Every new generation needs new approval; other features retain their own access requirements.

The dedicated DeepSeek test reads that built-in connection’s model list; other account validations may also send synthetic test content. Model refreshes and tests contact the corresponding configured address and send authentication information according to the protocol. These user-initiated operations are separate from generation approval and do not automatically select a different production account. A successful connection provides interface-level evidence, not completed rough-cut generation or editing-quality acceptance.

Failures never silently switch to another account or provider. Check the address, region, network, key validity, and provider balance. If the selected account/model was removed, its key is missing, or its model is not adapted, fix the configuration and select it again. Network requests may retry within limits; cancellation does not guarantee recall of sent data. Do not send keys to support. Share only the app’s sanitized error code, rather than a provider’s raw response.

Deletion and privacy requests

  • Remove the built-in DeepSeek local key with its dedicated Delete API Key control in Settings → AI & Models. Remove other keys within the corresponding Model Management account. Provider-side revocation is separate; local deletion does not guarantee requests already started stop.
  • Reset to Defaults restores ordinary preferences and default AI configuration and reselects built-in DeepSeek. Added accounts and Keychain keys are retained; project histories, bookmarks, and caches are not deleted.
  • Delete added accounts in Model Management to also delete their local credentials. A key-deletion failure retains the account for retry. The built-in DeepSeek account itself is protected; its key can still be removed through the dedicated control. If the selected account or model is deleted, choose a valid configuration again.
  • Manage source projects, media, and exports through Finder or similar tools. The app has no control to erase all local data or delete individual history entries. For manual removal of app state/caches, first quit the app and contact the privacy channel for guidance appropriate to your installation. Uninstalling does not establish that all data, Keychain items, or backups have been cleared.
  • For personal data already sent to DeepSeek’s official service, submit rights requests to privacy@deepseek.com; API assistance is available at api-service@deepseek.com. Model Studio users should use the official service and privacy channels applicable to their account; contact the actual operator of a custom service. TalkTrim cannot remotely delete these copies and does not guarantee approval of a request or completed deletion.

Trial and purchase support

TalkTrim offers a 14-day full-feature trial and a one-time Full Unlock, with no subscription or auto-renewal. Apple handles purchases and restoration. For store issues, provide the displayed error code and contact support. Do not send Apple passwords, payment-card details, or complete transaction credentials.

What to include when contacting us

Provide the TalkTrim, macOS, and Final Cut Pro versions, the steps that failed, and sanitized error codes. Redact personal information, project names, file paths, and account details from screenshots. Do not send API keys, passwords, private media, complete captions, or unredacted FCPXML. If a sample is needed, first agree on content rights and the minimum material required. Support correspondence enters your chosen support channel.

Support data is managed under a rule of retention for 90 days after the issue is resolved, except for applicable legal obligations and unresolved disputes. The developer manages this manually, without automatic cleanup. This does not confirm any completed deletion or guarantee complete deletion of email-provider backups. Ask about your data or request deletion through the email above. We do not promise a fixed response time.
